Subject: [PATCH 1/4] libc++abi: preserve foreign forced-unwind state
MIME-Version: 1.0
Content-Type: text/plain; charset=UTF-8
Content-Transfer-Encoding: 8bit

A foreign exception rethrown through __cxa_rethrow currently starts a new
unwind with _Unwind_RaiseException. For pthread cancellation this loses the
forced-unwind continuation. Use _Unwind_Resume_or_Rethrow for foreign
exceptions while retaining the existing native and SJLJ paths.

On ARM EHABI, the resumed forced unwind may reach a catching handler during
the cleanup phase. Treat a non-zero ttypeIndex as a catching handler and do
not register it through __cxa_begin_cleanup, matching libstdc++'s
found_cleanup gate.

The two changes form one series: applying only the rethrow change exposes the
EHABI cleanup-registration defect on ARM. Validation covers x86_64 native and
armv7l hardware with libgcc_s. It does not cover aarch64, LLVM libunwind,
concurrent cancellation, or mixed-runtime frame traversal.

中文说明：修复外来强制展开重抛状态丢失，并修复 ARM EHABI 将捕获
handler 误登记为 cleanup 的问题。两处必须作为同一系列应用。
